Room layout editor

Stage placement

Drag the stage rectangle. Fixtures remain where you placed them; stage-linked targets follow the stage. This changes the plan and targets, not a stage mesh in MagicVis.

Drag fixtures to position them. Select a fixture to enter exact coordinates and mounting rotations. Coordinates are relative to the room centre line at the rear of the stage; positive depth points toward the audience.

Layout positions are exported into MagicVis: X = left/right, Y = depth, Z = height. MagicQ stores positions to 0.01 m. Confirm rotations in MagicVis. Target-based focus is experimental and requires matching the fixture’s neutral beam direction, heading and pan/tilt direction.

Room targets

Drag a gold diamond to move a target. Guide lines show assigned fixtures converging on it. Target height sets the 3D convergence point. Regenerate and load the show to update aiming in MagicVis.

Selected fixture calibration

Enable target aiming after checking neutral beam and movement directions against MagicVis. Rotate floor fixtures to match their mounting. Unreachable targets stop export.

Beam height offset is the emitter height above/below the fixture position. Use small trims to correct a static miss; reduce circle size for movement overshoot.
