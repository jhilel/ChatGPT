ONE-TIME CONSOLE VALIDATION PACK

See manifest.json for the exact four fixture modes. These shows are development tests, not event files. They contain four fixtures and three Generic Dimmer target markers (heads 5-7). The markers are test aids, not part of a physical rig; keep their intensity at zero.

Load a show into MagicQ/MagicVis. Heads 1 and 2 hang at 4 m; heads 3 and 4 are upright at floor level. All are 8 m down the room. Stage height is 1 m. Verify the mounting and fixture personality before comparing beams.

1. Select heads 1-4 and open the Positions page. Compare generated focus with native PLOT -> FOCUS ON TARGET for marker 5 (centre). Keep the focus height at 1 m when comparing; a click alone may use the Plot's previous focus height.
2. Repeat for marker 6 (left, 1 m high) and marker 7 (audience point, 1.5 m high).
3. Check the stage-wash and mirrored-fan position cues. Beam centre targets should follow the plan; equal-looking footprints are not guaranteed for different mounting heights.
4. Check color controls, blackout and intensity pages. The intensity pages are HTP playbacks, not multiplicative group masters; release event looks to lower intensity using them.
5. Save any native reference cues and the resulting show, noting software version, fixture mode, pan/tilt inversion settings and focus height.

Fixture groups, playbacks, modes and geometry are generated; successful structural tests do not certify MagicVis or physical behavior. We need actual console observations before changing a mode's runtime-verification status.
