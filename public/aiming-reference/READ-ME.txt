ONE-TIME DEVELOPMENT TEST FOR AUTOMATIC AIMING

Use this separate test show, not your event show. Heads 1-4 are Maverick Storm 2 Profiles in 32ch mode. Heads 5-7 are Generic Dimmer target markers, not part of a real rig. All three target markers are at floor height.

1. Load Aiming Reference.shw into MagicQ. Stop playbacks and clear the programmer.
2. Open PLOT in overhead view. Select ONLY heads 1-4.
3. Press FOCUS ON TARGET, then click the CENTRE of head 5's marker in Plot. Set intensity and record REF CENTRE.
4. Clear the programmer, select only heads 1-4 again and repeat with head 6's marker. Record REF LEFT.
5. Repeat with head 7's marker. Record REF FAR.
6. Save as Aiming Reference Results.shw and upload it. Include a screenshot of the Plot window to establish the focus controls and view used. Do not move/rotate fixtures or manually adjust their pan/tilt.

Keep the focus height at floor level (0 metres) if a height control is shown. Clicking a marker identifies its X/Y location; it does not automatically select the marker's height. Tell us if MagicQ uses a different focus height or if the marker centres are hard to click accurately.

Coordinates are in metres (native MagicVis X,Y,Z):
Storm head 1: -3,8,4
Storm head 2: 3,8,4
Storm head 3: -3,8,0
Storm head 4: 3,8,0
Target head 5 (CENTRE): 0,2,0
Target head 6 (LEFT): -2,2,0
Target head 7 (FAR): 2,4,0

The mirrored fixture layout and three target positions allow independent checks of pan direction, tilt convention, mounting and beam-origin assumptions. This test does not validate other fixture modes or all physical mounting orientations.
